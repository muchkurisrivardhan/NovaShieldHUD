    How it works

    Three keys between you and anything

    1. 1

      Press Alt+Space

      The launcher drops in over whatever you're doing, ready to type. Alt+M opens it already listening.

    2. 2

      Type, or just say it

      Part of an app, file or window name, a sum, or a whole sentence like "turn the volume to 75 and dim the screen". Voice is transcribed on your PC.

    3. 3

      Enter

      It runs. Anything that closes, deletes or shuts down waits for a second keystroke first (on by default), whoever proposed it.

    Lock screen

    Your lock screen, in the same uniform

    Drawn at your monitor's real resolution and set through Windows' own lock screen settings. Signing in with your PIN, password or Windows Hello works exactly as before, and one click puts your old picture back.

    Account picture

    A padlock emblem that sits in the empty centre of the rings on the sign-in screen. Generated in every theme.

    Widgets

    Node status, day and year progress, and a countdown, for the Widgets board. Nothing private: no PC name, no network name, no notifications.

    Built in

    What NovaShield HUD does

    Six looks

    Tactical, HUD, Classic, Cyberpunk, Retro CRT and Minimal mono, plus any accent colour. The launcher, widgets and lock screen all switch together.

    Plain English

    "Turn the volume to 75 and dim the screen" runs as two steps, worked out on your PC without a model.

    Voice on your PC

    Speak instead of typing. Whisper transcribes on your machine; the audio never leaves it.

    Claude when you want it

    Start with ? for anything else. Claude can only use Beacon's own commands, and destructive ones ask you first.

    Focus and time

    A focus timer on your desktop, its countdown in the launcher, and time zones you can hover on the wallpaper map.

    Reversible by design

    Your original lock screen picture is saved before anything changes, and restored byte for byte.

    Private

    No account, no telemetry, no ads. Settings stay in your profile; your API key is encrypted with Windows.

    Light on your PC

    Ambient animations run at a low frame rate and shadows sit on static layers, so the widgets idle at a few percent of one core.

    No hacks

    No injection, no hooks, no patched system files. Only documented Windows features, and no admin rights for the apps.

    FAQ

    Questions

    Is it free?

    Yes. Downloading and using NovaShield HUD is free. Sponsors keep it that way.

    Does it send my data anywhere?

    No. There's no account and no telemetry. It only goes online when you ask Claude (with your own API key) and to fetch website icons for the launcher, which you can turn off.

    What does the lock screen part change?

    Only the lock screen picture, through the same setting Windows uses. Your sign-in, PIN, password and Windows Hello are untouched. Your old picture is saved first, and Restore puts it back.

    Does it need admin rights?

    The launcher, desktop widgets and lock screen picture don't. Installing the Widgets-board widgets asks once, to trust their signing certificate; uninstalling takes it away again.

    Which PCs does it run on?

    Windows 10 and Windows 11, on x64 (Intel and AMD) and ARM64 (Snapdragon) PCs.

    Can I see the code?

    Yes, it's public on GitHub. It isn't open source yet: all rights are reserved for now.
